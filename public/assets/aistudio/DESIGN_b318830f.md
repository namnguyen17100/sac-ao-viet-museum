## Brand & Style
This design system captures the scholarly serenity and dignified elegance of a Vietnamese digital museum and archival exhibition space. Rooted in imperial history, traditional silk craftsmanship, and ancient bronze and ceramics, the visual language marries historic reverence with contemporary curatorial clarity. 

The aesthetic adheres to **Curatorial Minimalism** with **Warm Tactile Underpinnings**:
- **Atmosphere:** Contemplative, scholarly, timeless, and reverent. Spaces feel like quiet exhibition halls illuminated by soft natural gallery light.
- **Visual Motif:** Generous white space reminiscent of silk mounting scrolls, ultra-fine framing borders recalling woodcut prints and lacquerware inlays, and understated typographic discipline.
- **Target Audience:** Historians, art collectors, cultural researchers, and global patrons seeking an immersive, authentic exploration of Vietnamese cultural artifacts.

## Colors
The color palette derives directly from traditional imperial craft, silk scrolls, and patinated antiquities:
- **Primary (`#522F3C` - Đỏ Rượu Cung Đình / Imperial Burgundy):** Applied to primary actions, key curatorial citations, active navigation indicators, and institutional seals. It embodies ceremonial weight and solemnity.
- **Secondary (`#AD7B58` - Nâu Đồng Cổ Điển / Antique Bronze):** Used for provenance metadata, secondary tags, structural borders, and refined iconography.
- **Tertiary (`#CA9A6C` - Hoàng Kim Nhạt / Muted Royal Gold):** Reserved for focal accents, rare artifact badges, accession highlights, and subtle ornamental divider rules.
- **Neutral Primary (`#2C2825` - Than Cổ / Deep Charcoal):** Primary body and display text color, avoiding harsh digital blacks in favor of an ink-stone softness.
- **Neutral Muted (`#5C5650` - Ghi Ấm / Warm Slate):** Captions, secondary timestamps, copyright declarations, and catalog numbers.
- **Surface Roles:**
  - Canvas Base: `#F9F6EF` (Ngà Cổ / Aged Ivory) provides warmth across full-screen layouts.
  - Card & Container Surface: `#FFFDF7` (Trắng Giấy Lụa / Silk Paper White) offers subtle separation without harsh contrast.
  - Border Tint: `#E6DFD3` (Sợi Gai / Muted Woven Fiber) for hairline dividers and museum-grade containment.

## Typography
Typography reflects an editorial, curatorial balance:
- **Headings & Historical Citations (`Noto Serif`):** Delivers literary dignity and impeccable Vietnamese diacritical rendering. Titles carry an unhurried, measured pace.
- **Narrative & Interface Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Warm, open, highly legible sans-serif geometry that prevents visual fatigue across long historical narratives and catalog databases.
- **Labels & Classification Codes:** Rendered in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to evoke museum archive drawers and curated taxonomy plaques.
- **Academic Captions:** A dedicated serif sub-style for artifact descriptions, year of origin, and medium classifications.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout simulates an open-air curatorial pavilion:
- **Grid Structure:** A 12-column responsive fluid grid with wide gutters (`2.5rem` on desktop) ensuring artifacts are not overcrowded.
- **Rhythm:** Asymmetric whitespace gives breathing room to single focal artifacts, echoing the composition of traditional scroll paintings.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (1280px+):** Deep outer margins (`margin-lg: 5rem`), dual-panel catalog views (artifact viewport paired with persistent academic commentary).
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** Balanced margins (`margin-md: 3rem`), switching multi-column exhibits into paired modular cards.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Compact fluid margins (`margin: 1.5rem`), single-column progression preserving vertical reading cadence.

## Elevation & Depth
In alignment with historical parchment and museum conservation standards, digital skeuomorphic drop shadows are prohibited:
- **Tonal Layering:** Depth is communicated through calibrated surface hierarchy: Base Canvas (`#F9F6EF`) sits beneath Silk Paper White (`#FFFDF7`) containers.
- **Framing & Hairlines:** Containers feature crisp `1px` solid borders tinted with `#E6DFD3`. Focused or hovered cards shift their border to Antique Bronze (`#AD7B58`).
- **Diffuse Ambient Glow:** For interactive overlays, modals, and enlarged artifact viewports, elevation is achieved via a warm amber-diffused haze (`rgba(82, 47, 60, 0.04)` blur radius `24px`) rather than a heavy black shadow.

## Shapes
A restrained curvature of `0.25rem` (soft) governs the design system. 
- Rectangular architectural foundations reference historic pavilion floor plans and antique mounting plaques.
- Minor radius rounding (`roundedness: 1`) softens harsh digital vectors while preserving an authentic, paper-cut crispness.
- Decorative museum seals, era tags, and copyright verification shields maintain strict geometric discipline without transitioning into modern pill shapes.

## Components

### Buttons & Curatorial Triggers
- **Primary Button:** Background in Imperial Burgundy (`#522F3C`), label in Silk Paper White (`#FFFDF7`), `1px` border in `#522F3C`, `0.25rem` border radius. Hover introduces a transition to `#3D212B` with a subtle interior frame highlight.
- **Secondary (Provenance) Button:** Transparent background, `1px` solid border in Antique Bronze (`#AD7B58`), text in Deep Charcoal (`#2C2825`). Hover softly washes the background with `#F4EFE6`.
- **Text Link:** Underlined with a delicate hairline in Muted Royal Gold (`#CA9A6C`), offset by `4px`.

### Artifact Exhibition Cards
- **Structure:** Solid `#FFFDF7` background wrapped in a `1px` `#E6DFD3` border.
- **Image Treatment:** Inset by `space-sm` padding with high-fidelity archival imagery set against neutral light background. 
- **Metadata Layout:** Dynasty/Era tag sits at the top right in `label-sm`; title in `Noto Serif` `headline-sm`; accession and inventory code in `caption-academic`.

### Classification Badges & Era Chips
- **Style:** Rectilinear tags with `0.25rem` corners.
- **Palette:** `#F4EFE6` ground, `#5C5650` typography, framed with `#DFD7C8` border.
- **Rare Artifact Indicator:** `#FFFDF7` ground accented with a left-edge `2px` strip in Muted Royal Gold (`#CA9A6C`).

### Academic Inputs & Exhibition Search
- **Search Fields:** Inset field style with `#FFFDF7` surface, subtle `#D9D0C1` bottom border (or full bounding hairline), placeholder in Warm Slate (`#5C5650`).
- **Focus State:** Border shifts cleanly to `#AD7B58` without high-contrast glow rings.

### Copyright & Provenance Disclosure Notices
- **Layout:** Dedicated archival banner or bottom-docked panel rendered in `#F4EFE6` with an accompanying seal icon.
- **Typography:** `body-sm` font, explicitly outlining attribution rights, digitization credits, museum acquisition numbers, and open cultural data licensing.

### Museum Gallery Navigation
- **Header:** Translucent Aged Ivory backdrop (`#F9F6EF` at 92% opacity with minimal backdrop blur), bordered below by a `1px` gold-tinted hairline (`#E8DFD1`).
- **Chronology Timeline Scrubber:** Horizontal track in `#E6DFD3` punctuated by diamond or square epoch nodes in `#AD7B58`, expanding on hover to reveal dynasty intervals.
